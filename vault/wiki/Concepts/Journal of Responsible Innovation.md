# Journal of Responsible Innovation

The Journal of Responsible Innovation (JRI) is a scholarly journal focused on the principles and practices of responsible innovation, particularly in the context of complex technological developments.

## Key Details

- JRI’s focus includes governance, practice, and assessment of knowledge-based innovation.
- The journal emphasizes anticipation, reflection, inclusion, and responsiveness.
